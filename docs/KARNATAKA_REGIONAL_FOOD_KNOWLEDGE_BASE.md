# Karnataka Regional Food Knowledge Base & Culinary Geography

## 1. Culinary Architecture Overview

Karnataka possesses one of India's most diverse culinary landscapes, shaped by distinct agricultural belts, coastal maritime traditions, royal palace cuisines, and ancient Deccan culinary practices.

Namma Karnataka categorizes food into **8 authentic culinary regions**, ensuring regional itineraries feature genuine local specialties rather than generic North Indian or generic South Indian dishes.

---

## 2. The 8 Distinct Regional Cuisines

### 1. Bengaluru Urban & Rural
* **Culinary Identity**: Legendary Darshini & Brahmin Tiffin Room culture with degree filter coffee.
* **Signature Dishes**:
  * *Crispy Butter Masala Dosa*: Thin golden-crisp crepe smeared with spicy red garlic chutney and seasoned potato palya.
  * *Bisi Bele Bath*: Slow-cooked rice-lentil stew enriched with tamarind, fresh ghee, seasonal veggies, and khara boondi.
  * *Chow Chow Bath*: Paired sweet kesari bath and savory vegetable khara bath.
  * *Thatte Idli (Bidadi / Kyatsandra)*: Plate-sized steamed spongy idli with red coconut chutney and butter.
* **Venues**: Darshinis, Heritage Tiffin Rooms (Vidyarthi Bhavan, CTR, Taaza Thindi).
* **Cost Range**: ₹60 – ₹140 per meal.

### 2. Mysuru & Mandya (Old Mysore)
* **Culinary Identity**: Royal Wadiyar palace delicacies and agrarian country fare.
* **Signature Dishes**:
  * *Ragi Mudde with Nati Koli Saaru / Bassaru*: Finger-millet dumpling served with spicy country chicken curry or traditional dill greens broth.
  * *Maddur Vada*: Crunchy, onion-infused semolina flat-patty born at Maddur railway station.
  * *Mysore Pak*: Melt-in-mouth gram-flour and pure ghee royal confection.
  * *Melukote Puliyogare*: Temple tamarind rice tempered with dried coconut, peanuts, and aromatic roasted spices.
* **Venues**: Authentic Military Hotels, Country Messes, Temple Bhojana Shalas.
* **Cost Range**: ₹80 – ₹260 per meal.

### 3. Coastal Karavali (Canara & Tulunadu)
* **Culinary Identity**: Coconut, fresh Arabian sea catch, tamarind, and Byadgi chillies.
* **Signature Dishes**:
  * *Neer Dosa with Kori Rotti / Kundapura Chicken*: Paper-thin delicate water crepes served with spicy coconut curry.
  * *Anjal (Kingfish) Tawa Fry*: Fresh fish pan-fried with red chilli-vinegar marinade.
  * *Mangalore Goli Baje*: Soft, fluffy sour curd and ginger fritters.
  * *Pathrode*: Colocasia leaves smeared with spicy-sweet spiced gram paste and steamed.
* **Venues**: Coastal Seafood Shacks, Udupi Brahmin Canteens, Canara Family Messes.
* **Cost Range**: ₹100 – ₹280 per meal.

### 4. Malnad (Western Ghats Sahyadri)
* **Culinary Identity**: High-range plantation produce, jackfruit, wild bamboo shoots, and wild mangoes.
* **Signature Dishes**:
  * *Akki Rotti*: Rice-flour flatbread roasted on iron griddles with fresh dill, coconut, and cumin.
  * *Halasina Hannina Kadubu*: Steamed fragrant jackfruit dumplings wrapped in teak or banana leaves.
  * *Baimbale Curry*: Tender bamboo shoot curry prepared during the monsoons.
  * *Kaadu Maavina Saaru*: Tangy sweet-and-sour wild mango curry.
* **Venues**: Coffee Estate Homestays, High-Range Local Eateries.
* **Cost Range**: ₹80 – ₹180 per meal.

### 5. Kodagu (Coorg Highlands)
* **Culinary Identity**: Kodava warrior feasts sour-tarted with garcinia (Kachampuli vinegar) and black pepper.
* **Signature Dishes**:
  * *Kadambuttu with Pandi Curry*: Pillowy steamed broken-rice spheres with dark roasted spice meat curry.
  * *Noolputtu*: Hand-pressed steamed rice string hoppers with coconut-jaggery milk.
  * *Kummu (Wild Mushroom) Curry*: Seasonal forest mushrooms stewed with roasted cumin and coconut.
* **Venues**: Kodava Heritage Homestays, Madikeri Local Dining Rooms.
* **Cost Range**: ₹140 – ₹350 per meal.

### 6. North Karnataka (Jowar Heartland)
* **Culinary Identity**: Low-glycemic sorghum flatbreads, sesame, groundnuts, and fiery country gravies.
* **Signature Dishes**:
  * *Jolada Rotti Oota with Ennegayi*: Fire-baked unleavened sorghum bread with stuffed small brinjals in peanut-sesame gravy, served with shenga chutney pudi and curds.
  * *Pithla / Junka*: Spiced gram flour porridge seasoned with mustard and garlic.
  * *Dharwad Peda & Belagavi Kunda*: Slow-caramelized milk confections.
* **Venues**: Traditional Lingayat Khanavalis, Basaveshwara Dining Halls.
* **Cost Range**: ₹80 – ₹160 per meal.

### 7. Kalyana Karnataka (Deccan Heartland)
* **Culinary Identity**: Medieval Bahmani and Nizam court recipes blending Persian spices with local pulses.
* **Signature Dishes**:
  * *Kalaburagi Tahari*: One-pot fragrant spiced long-grain rice with marinated meat or spiced potatoes.
  * *Bidar Naan Qalia*: Soft, fluffy clay-oven leavened bread paired with slow-simmered rich gravy.
  * *Jowar Sankati with Hulisoppu Saaru*: Sorghum porridge served with wild sour-greens lentils.
* **Venues**: Kalyana Regional Dining Halls, Market Messes.
* **Cost Range**: ₹90 – ₹220 per meal.

### 8. Central Karnataka (Tungabhadra Belt)
* **Culinary Identity**: River basin breakfast staples and crisp snacks.
* **Signature Dishes**:
  * *Davanagere Benne Dosa*: Wood-fired crispy cast-iron dosa basted with fresh white unsalted butter, served with potato palya and spicy coconut chutney.
  * *Chitradurga Menasinakai Bonda*: Batter-fried spiced long green chillies.
  * *Mandakki Oggarane*: Seasoned puffed rice snack garnished with roasted gram and fresh coriander.
* **Venues**: Original Benne Dosa Stalls, Highway Tea Canteens.
* **Cost Range**: ₹50 – ₹120 per meal.

## 3. Dietary Safety & Filtering Engine

The `FoodRecommendationService` applies multi-criteria dietary filtering:
* `veg`: Excludes all poultry, meat, fish, and seafood.
* `vegan`: Excludes all dairy, ghee, curd, and animal products.
* `jain`: Excludes root vegetables (onions, garlic, potatoes, carrots).
* `no_seafood`: Accommodates travelers with shellfish or fish allergies while permitting poultry and vegetarian items.
