## 2. District Distribution & Coverage Expansion

With this inventory, the application eliminates regional blind spots:
* **Coastal Karnataka (Udupi & Uttara Kannada)**: Maravanthe coastal highway, Barkur temple ruins, Kudle Beach, Yana geological rock spires.
* **Malenadu & Western Ghats (Shivamogga & Hassan)**: Kavaledurga rainforest bastion, Jog Falls, Shettihalli Gothic waters, Doddagaddavalli Chatuskuta Hoysala shrine.
* **Kalyana Karnataka & North Karnataka (Bidar, Gadag, Bagalkote)**: Mahmud Gawan medieval Persianate college, Lakkundi Kalyani stepwells, Aihole and Pattadakal Chalukyan crucibles.
* **Old Mysuru (Mandya & Kodagu)**: Tonnur Kere crystalline glacial lake, Talakaveri sacred springs.
* **Central Karnataka (Chitradurga & Chikkaballapura)**: Chitradurga's 7-tier granite stone fortress, Bhoga Nandeeshwara 9th-century Nolamba-Chola-Hoysala complex.

## 3. Financial & Tariff Profile

Every discovered destination includes verified or strictly bounded entry fee ranges:
* **Zero Entry Fee (Free Public Heritage & Nature)**: 62.5% of discovered places (Kavaledurga, Tonnur Kere, Shettihalli Church, Maravanthe Beach, Kudle Beach, Yana Rocks, Bhoga Nandeeshwara).
* **Nominal ASI Conservation Tariff (₹25/person)**: Doddagaddavalli, Lakkundi Musukina Bavi, Aihole Durga complex, Chitradurga Fort, Pattadakal.
* **Budget Optimization Role**: These zero- and low-fee destinations form the core building blocks used by `BudgetOptimizer` to swap out expensive commercial attractions when bringing itineraries under strict budget ceilings.
